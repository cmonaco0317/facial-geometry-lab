        04 / THE ENGINEERING

        Small contracts.
        Honest boundaries.

        Validate at the boundary

        Finite, bounded coordinates. Explicit missing anchors. Invalid dimensions and stale revisions are rejected.

        Preserve the evidence

        Manual readings have versioned definitions and source contracts. Missing anchors disable only dependent measurements.

        Separate the claims

        Two-dimensional geometry is descriptive. This public edition includes no automatic photo analysis, clinical diagnosis or calibrated beauty model.
